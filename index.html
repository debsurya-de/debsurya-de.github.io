Research

Johns Hopkins University
Baltimore, Maryland

My research is in probability, high-dimensional statistics, machine learning, and theoretical computer science. I am particularly interested in problems where statistical estimation is possible in principle but may be computationally difficult.

I use low-degree polynomial methods, concentration inequalities, random matrix theory, and combinatorial techniques. My recent work studies low-rank estimation under inhomogeneous noise.

My advisor is Professor Dmitriy (Tim) Kunisky. I expect to graduate in September 2027.
