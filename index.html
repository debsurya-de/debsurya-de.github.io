I’m a PhD candidate at Johns Hopkins University. I study the mathematics of statistical inference and develop algorithms and software for analyzing complex data, often in collaboration with biomedical researchers.

Research

In high-dimensional statistics, I study how much data and computation a problem requires. I use tools from probability and theoretical computer science to understand the limits of efficient algorithms.

I also develop methods for combining data from different sources, clustering sequences, and solving constrained optimization problems. In biomedical collaborations, I’ve used these tools to study treatment histories and combine predictors of clinical outcomes.

My advisor is Professor Dmitriy (Tim) Kunisky. I expect to graduate in September 2027.
